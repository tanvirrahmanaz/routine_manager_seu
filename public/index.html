Weekly Schedule (Friday – Thursday)

This schedule is generated automatically from the department's recurring weekly routine (Friday through Thursday) and stays active for as long as the semester runs — whether that's 6 months, 8 months, or otherwise — until you import a new routine. Friday is shown as two separate tables — Friday (LLB) for the day-time rooms and Friday (LLM) for the evening room with its own time slots and the Friday Prayer break — matching the department's actual routine sheet. When adding or deleting a class you can choose whether it applies only to that single date or to every occurrence of that weekday going forward (e.g. every Wednesday). Changes are saved to this browser.
